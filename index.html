📦 3D Box Stacking

Pick up the boxes and stack them in the yellow build zone exactly like the see-through plan shows. Sit about an arm's length from the laptop so the camera sees both hands.

✋📦🤚 Hands on both sides of a box, then squeeze in = lift it (outlines show where)
📦 Move your hands = carry it · the box goes where you point
👐 Move your hands apart = put it down
🚶 Point at a far box, or carry a box over the build zone, and hold still = walk there
↩️ Hands at the left / right edge = turn · hands on the yellow arrow = go to the next box
Mouse: hold on a box to carry it · click a far box or the yellow arrow to walk to it · W A S D or arrows = walk / turn · P = pause · M = sound
