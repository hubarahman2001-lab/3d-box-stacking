📦 3D Box Stacking

Walk around the room, pick up the boxes and stack them in the build zone exactly like the see-through plan shows. Sit or stand so the camera sees your face and both hands.

🙌 One hand each side of a box, bring them together = lift it
📦 Move both hands = move the box · tilt them = tilt it
🫸 Hands closer to / further from the camera = box nearer / deeper
👐 Move your hands apart = put it down
🧍 Lean left / right = turn · lean in = walk forward · lean back = walk back
Mouse: hold to carry · W A S D or arrows = walk / turn · wheel or Q / E = depth
